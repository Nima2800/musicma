# Musicma design plan

## Subject
Musicma — live listening room for songs indexed from Telegram channels. Audience: Persian listeners. Page job: discover a channel vibe and start playback immediately.

## Palette
- `ink` `#0E1320` — deep navy night booth
- `vinyl` `#1A2238` — elevated surface
- `mist` `#C8D0E0` — body text
- `signal` `#F2EDE4` — brand / titles
- `filament` `#E8A54B` — warm amber accent (lamp over the desk)
- `bloom` `#5B7CFA` — soft indigo secondary for waveform / active rows

## Type
- Display: **Syne** — geometric energy for the Musicma wordmark
- Body: **Vazirmatn** — Persian-first UI text

## Layout
Full-bleed atmospheric hero; brand is the thesis. Channel chips and track rows live below the fold — no hero cards, no stat strips.

## Signature
Animated equalizer waveform behind / beside the Musicma wordmark — the “night booth is already playing” moment.

## Critique notes
Avoided cream+terracotta, acid-green-on-black, and broadsheet hairlines. Risk is the filament amber + blooming waveform in a navy booth, specific to late-night radio listening rather than generic music dashboards.
